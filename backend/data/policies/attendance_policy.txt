# Title: Attendance & Working Hours Policy
# Document ID: DOC-007
# Category: Work Arrangements
# Version: 1.2
# Effective Date: January 1, 2025

SECTION 1: WORKING HOURS & FLEXIBILITY
1.1 Core Business Hours: All employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaborative work during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
1.2 Flexible Start/End: Bandwidth allows start times between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided standard 8-hour workday requirements are fulfilled.

SECTION 2: ABSENCE NOTIFICATION
2.1 Unexpected Absence: In case of illness or emergency, employees must notify their direct manager and HR by email or Slack no later than 2 hours prior to their planned start time (or by 8:00 AM standard time).
2.2 Unexcused Absence: Three consecutive days of unexcused absence without communication is treated as voluntary resignation.
