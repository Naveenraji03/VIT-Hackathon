# Title: Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

# Category: Work Arrangements
# Version: 1.5
# Effective Date: February 1, 2025

SECTION 1: HYBRID WORK ELIGIBILITY
1.1 Minimum Service Tenure: Full-time employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service are eligible to apply for hybrid work arrangements.
1.2 Working Schedule: Eligible staff may work remotely up to 2 days per week (typically Mondays and Fridays) with prior manager approval.
1.3 Core Presence Days: Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays are mandatory in-office collaboration days for all engineering and product teams.

SECTION 2: WORKSPACE & EQUIPMENT
2.1 Home Office Setup Allowance: A one-time stipend of $500 USD is provided for ergonomic chair and monitor setup.
2.2 Security Requirements: Remote work must occur on company-managed devices connected via mandatory corporate VPN. Working from public Wi-Fi without VPN is strictly prohibited.
