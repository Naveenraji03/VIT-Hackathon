SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE ALLOCATION (2025)
1.1 Total Entitlement: Full-time employees are entitled to 18 business days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.2 Accrual Rate: Leave accrues at the rate of 1.5 days per completed calendar month of service.
1.3 Carryover Limit: Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any unused leave exceeding 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE & PERSONAL LEAVE (2025)
2.1 Sick Days: Employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave annually. A doctor's note is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
2.2 Maternity & Paternity Leave: Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 2 weeks.

SECTION 3: REQUEST & APPROVAL PROCESS
3.1 Notice Period: Annual leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance through the HR Portal.
