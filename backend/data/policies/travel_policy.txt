SECTION 1: BUSINESS TRAVEL ELIGIBILITY & APPROVAL
1.1 Approval: All business travel must be pre-approved by a Department Director at least 14 days prior to departure.
1.2 Flight Booking: Domestic flights must be booked in Economy Class. Transatlantic/International flights over 8 hours permit Economy Plus or Premium Economy.

SECTION 2: MEAL PER DIEM & ACCOMMODATION
2.1 Daily Meal Per-Diem: The standard daily meal per-diem allowance for domestic business travel is $75 USD per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner). Receipts are NOT required for standard per-diem claims under $75.
2.2 Accommodation Limits: Hotel bookings must not exceed $250 USD per night in standard tier cities, or $350 USD per night in designated high-cost metropolitan areas (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo).
