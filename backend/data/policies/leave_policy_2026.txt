SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE ALLOCATION (2026)
1.1 Total Entitlement: Effective January 1, 2026, all full-time employees are entitled to 20 business days of paid annual leave per calendar year (an increase from the previous 18 days in 2025).
1.2 Accrual Rate: Leave accrues at the rate of 1.66 days per completed calendar month of service.
1.3 Carryover Limit: Employees may carry over a maximum of 7 unused leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any unused leave exceeding 7 days is forfeited on December 31.

SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE & WELLNESS (2026)
2.1 Sick Days: Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave annually, plus 2 dedicated Mental Health/Wellness Days.
2.2 Medical Certificate: A doctor's note is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days.
2.3 Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.

SECTION 3: REQUEST & PRECEDENCE
3.1 Approval: Requests must be submitted via the HR Portal.
3.2 Policy Conflict Precedence Rule: In any conflict between prior policy documents (including Leave Policy 2025) and this 2026 Edition, the 2026 Edition (DOC-003) takes absolute precedence for all active staff as of Jan 1, 2026.
