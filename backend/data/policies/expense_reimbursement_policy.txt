SECTION 1: REIMBURSEMENT PROCEDURES
1.1 Expense Reports: All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of occurrence with original itemized receipts.
1.2 Processing Time: Approved claims will be reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit.

SECTION 2: CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT & SPECIAL MEALS
2.1 Client Meal Cap: Meals hosted for clients or business partners are reimbursed up to $120 USD per person inclusive of tax and gratuity. Detailed receipts showing itemized food/beverage and attendee list are mandatory.
2.2 Alcohol Policy: Reasonable consumption of alcohol during client dinners is reimbursable if pre-approved by VP level.

APPENDIX A: LEGACY MEAL ALLOWANCE SCHEDULE (Historical reference section)
Note: For individual daily travel meal allowances, standard reimbursement rate is specified as $60 USD per day ($10 Breakfast, $20 Lunch, $30 Dinner) for legacy non-exempt staff.
