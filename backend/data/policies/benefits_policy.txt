SECTION 1: HEALTH & DENTAL INSURANCE
1.1 Coverage Commencement: Full-time employees are eligible for health and dental insurance effective on Day 1 of employment.
1.2 Dental Allowance: Dental insurance covers 100% of preventive care and up to $1,500 USD per calendar year for major dental procedures per covered individual.
1.3 Vision Care: Annual eye exams are covered 100%, with $200 USD biannual hardware allowance for prescription glasses/contacts.

SECTION 2: RETIREMENT & MATCHING
2.1 401(k) Plan: Company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 5% of annual base salary. Immediate 100% vesting applies.
