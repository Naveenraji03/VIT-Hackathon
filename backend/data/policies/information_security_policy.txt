SECTION 1: PASSWORD & ACCESS CONTROL
1.1 Password Requirements: Corporate account passwords must be at least 14 characters in length, containing uppercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Passwords expire every 90 days.
1.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): MFA is compulsory for all external logins, single sign-on (SSO), and VPN connections.

SECTION 2: AI TOOLS & DATA CLASSIFICATION
2.1 Use of Public Generative AI Tools: Employees are prohibited from inputting Confidential or Proprietary company data into unapproved public AI tools.
2.2 Data Classification: Data is classified into Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Financial records and employee PII are classified as Restricted.
